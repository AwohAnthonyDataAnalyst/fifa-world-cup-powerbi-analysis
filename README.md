# FIFA World Cup Power BI Analysis (2002–2018)

## Project Overview

This capstone project analyzes FIFA World Cup national team performance across five tournament cycles (2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, and 2018).

The solution was developed using Power BI, Power Query, DAX, and a star-schema data model to answer key business questions related to team performance, scoring trends, wins, losses, goal differences, and tournament outcomes.



## Business Problem

A sports analytics organization requires insights into national team performance across multiple FIFA World Cup tournaments.

The objective is to transform raw tournament data into a decision-ready business intelligence solution that enables stakeholders to:

- Evaluate country performance
- Identify top and bottom-performing teams
- Analyze scoring trends
- Compare team performance over time
- Support data-driven decision making



## Dataset Description

The dataset contains FIFA World Cup national team performance data for:

- FIFA World Cup 2002
- FIFA World Cup 2006
- FIFA World Cup 2010
- FIFA World Cup 2014
- FIFA World Cup 2018

Metrics analyzed include:

- Games Played
- Wins
- Draws
- Losses
- Goals For
- Goals Against
- Goal Difference
- Points

## Key Findings

1. Germany achieved the highest overall goal difference across the five tournaments.

2. Brazil accumulated the highest total points among participating countries.

3. Total goals scored fluctuated across tournaments rather than increasing consistently.

4. The highest-scoring tournament was 2014 with 171 total goals scored.

5. Several countries demonstrated strong consistency across multiple World Cup cycles.

---

## Recommendations

1. Focus scouting and performance benchmarking on consistently high-performing teams such as Germany and Brazil.

2. Use goal difference alongside points when evaluating overall tournament effectiveness.

3. Monitor long-term tournament trends rather than relying on single-year performance.
